## Description (max 4000 characters)
Teeter is a one-tap balance game: a block swings back and forth above your tower, and you decide the exact moment to drop it.

Land it dead center on the block below for a PERFECT and keep the full width. Miss the timing and the overhang gets cut off, narrowing your base for the next drop. Miss the tower completely and it all comes down — back to zero.

Easy to grasp, hard to put down:
• One tap, no hidden rules
• The tower narrows with every imprecise drop — every tap counts
• Chain PERFECTs into streaks for bonus points
• The pace ramps up gradually — calm start, then it tests your reflexes
• Your best score is saved automatically on your device
• Sound and haptic feedback on every drop, toggle them off in Settings

No account required, no personal data collected — just play.
