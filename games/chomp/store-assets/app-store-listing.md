## Description (max 4000 characters)
You are a small herbivore in a 13×13 clearing, and you never stop running.

You don't move your dino. You point it. Swipe up, down, left or right and it takes the next square in that direction, on its own clock — so every run is about reading two squares ahead instead of reacting.

Eat the amber frond for +1. Another one grows somewhere else. Do it again.

Except you are not alone out there. One T-Rex hunts you across the clearing, and it is the only thing on the board that wants you dead on purpose. Trees block your path and cost you a step you cannot afford. Water ends the run instantly. And every so often the Rex simply leaves the board — which is your one moment to breathe, right up until the footprints appear on an edge you weren't watching and it comes charging back in.

Both of you get faster the more you eat. He gets faster quicker.

• Swipe to steer — one input, no buttons, no hidden rules
• A new clearing every run: trees, ponds and food are never in the same place twice
• One T-Rex, always. It leaves the board and re-enters somewhere new
• Two ways to die: his jaws, or your own footing
• Your best score is saved automatically on your device
• Sound and haptic feedback, toggle them off in Settings

No account required, no personal data collected.
