## Description (max 4000 characters)
Dialed is a one-tap precision game: a needle sweeps back and forth across a dial, and you tap the instant it lands inside the zone.

Land dead center for a PERFECT and keep the run alive at full value. Chain PERFECTs back-to-back to build a streak bonus. Miss the zone entirely and the run is over.

Easy to grasp, hard to master:
• One tap, no hidden rules
• Land dead center for a PERFECT — chain them into a streak bonus
• The zone narrows and the pace quickens gradually, so the first runs are forgiving and mastery is earned over time
• Your best score is saved automatically on your device
• Sound and haptic feedback on every tap, toggle them off in Settings

No account required, no personal data collected — just get dialed in.
