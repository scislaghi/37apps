## Description (4000 char max)
Juke is a one-thumb reflex game: hexes rush toward you, each carrying an arrow. Swipe the way it points before it reaches you.

Simple — until the feints start. Hexes marked (!) are lying: swipe the opposite way instead, or they catch you out. Read fast, commit faster, and keep your streak alive to earn bonus time on the clock.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe left or right to match the arrow
• Watch for (!) feint hexes — juke the opposite direction
• Chain correct swipes to build a streak and bank extra time
• One wrong move ends the run — beat your best score

WHY YOU'LL KEEP COMING BACK
• Pick-up-and-play in seconds, no tutorial needed
• Escalating pace keeps every run tense
• Clean, colorful, distraction-free design
• Your best score is always one swipe away from being beaten

No accounts, no pay-to-win, no timers between runs. Just you, your reflexes, and the next hex.
