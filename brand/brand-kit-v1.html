04

Icon system

This is what actually needs to exist right now, before any game exists — not final artwork, the rulebook a future icon must follow.

Why

The icon is the single highest-leverage asset you'll ship — it's the thing a stranger judges in a search grid before anything else, and it's most of what makes them tap. Locking the system now (not the art) means every future game gets designed the same disciplined way: bold single silhouette, readable at 60px, no wasted detail — instead of re-deciding this from scratch 15 times.

What makes each game its own

Your point is the right one — same skeleton everywhere, but a game with zero personality beyond a color swap will feel like a reskin, and reskins don't build a fanbase. Three levers stay fixed, three stay free per game:

"Signature motif" = one small recurring shape or effect, unique to that game, reused across its own menu / transitions / particles — the glow behind the icon in the Menu mockup below is Skyhop's version. A future game could use a scatter pattern, a trail effect, or a distinct button shape instead — as long as it's the same three screens underneath.

Sound identity

TBD

Not demoable here, but the direction to lock: short, dry, pitched SFX — a light tap/click, a rising arpeggio on score-up, a soft low thud on game-over. No music loop required for v1; keep the whole soundscape under ~5 shared samples reused across every game so it stays cheap to produce and instantly recognizable as "a 37apps game."

Open for your feedback

Dark base, really?
Proposed as default since it makes accent colors pop and suits arcade energy — happy to flip to a light neutral base instead if you'd rather.
8 accents enough?
Covers ~15 games with repeats spaced apart. Can extend the formula (same saturation/lightness) to add more anytime.
Fonts feel right?
ui-rounded + Avenir Next are system fonts (free, no licensing). If you want something more custom we can pick a licensed pairing instead.
