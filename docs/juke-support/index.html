Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to match the direction each hex's arrow points before it reaches you. Hexes marked with ⚠ are a feint — juke the opposite way instead. Chaining correct swipes keeps your clock topped up.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, your best score is saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will reset it.

How do I turn off sound or haptics?

Tap the speaker icon in the top-right corner of the start screen, or go to Settings inside the app to toggle sound and haptics independently.
