Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag up or down anywhere on screen to morph your shape between cube, tall, and wide. Match the shape to the gap in each gate before it reaches you — the wrong shape on contact ends the run.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, your best score is saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will reset it.

How do I turn off sound?

Tap the speaker icon in the top-right corner of the start screen, or go to Settings inside the app.
