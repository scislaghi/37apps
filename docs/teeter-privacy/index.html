Teeter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Teeter ("the app") is a mobile game developed by Simone Cislaghi. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

No account, no personal data collected by us

Teeter does not require sign-up or login, and does not collect your name, email, or any personal information directly. Your best score is stored only on your device.

Advertising

Teeter shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support free access to the game. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier / IDFA)
  • Usage and interaction data (such as ad impressions and clicks)
  • Approximate location, where permitted

On iOS, the app requests App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking-related identifier is used, as required by Apple. You can allow or deny this at any time from your device Settings.

This data is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and Google's User Consent Policy.

Children's privacy

Teeter is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset ad personalization or tracking permissions from your device's system settings at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data (such as your best score).

Contact
